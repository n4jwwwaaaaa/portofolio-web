## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-energy, tactile workshop aesthetic themed after a designer's physical workstation. Drawing inspiration from desktop cutting mats, drafting grids, spiral notepads, physical binders, and adhesive stationery, the system shifts digital interaction into an expressive, physical craft space.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Vibrant & Inventive:** Evoking creative momentum, hands-on crafting, and visual energy.
- **Approachable & Playful:** Replacing corporate sterility with warm stationary metaphors, tactile stationery clips, stickers, and crisp drop-shadows.
- **Meticulous & Structured:** Grounded by technical engineering precision, mathematical grid underlays, and disciplined typography.

### Design Movement
The system balances **Tactile Neopop / Scrapbook Skeuomorphism** with contemporary web usability:
- Physical textures (graph paper, spiral punched holes, torn margins, wood grain rims).
- Hard offset shadows paired with crisp outlines.
- Playful tool stickers, bounding-box selection chrome, and physical pinning accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive work desk environment anchored by professional cutting-mat blue, warm organic desk trim, crisp notepad sheets, and vibrant stationery highlights.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Primary (`#1D63B8`) & Deep Mat (`#144B91`):** Serves as the primary viewport surface—representing self-healing rotary cutting mats. Imprinted with translucent grid markings (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`).
- **Secondary (`#FFD23F`):** Vivid yellow applied to sticky notes, active primary focus states, folder icons, and attention-grabbing badge ribbons.
- **Paper & Workspace Bases (`#F8F7F3` / `#FFFFFF`):** Authentic drafting, spiral-bound, and graph-lined card stock surfaces for legibility.
- **Wood Accents (`#A67B5B`, `#B88655`, `#7C532E`):** Warm desk surfaces and clipboard bases framing the cutting mat layout.
- **Stationery Accents (`#FF5CA8`, `#38BDF8`):** Pink binder clips, pixel status chips, taped highlights, and cyan wireframe selection borders.
- **Text & Structure (`#18181B`, `#52525B`):** Archival ink blacks providing maximum contrast for typography, crisp borders, and hard directional drop shadows.

## Typography

The type system blends structural clarity with visual accents to maintain readability while supporting the tactile stationery narrative.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` with heavy weights (700, 800) delivers clear, geometric rhythm for headlines and titles. Bounding box titles incorporate white sticker borders and drop-shadow outlines for a pop-out effect.
- **Editorial & Body Text:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (500) ensures reading comfort across dense multi-column timelines, job descriptions, and project overviews.
- **Accent Styles:**
  - Pixel Badges & Micro Stamps: Stylized pixel typography (`Silkscreen` / `Press Start 2P`) for retro tags, year indicators (`2026`), and release tokens.
  - Handwritten Desk Marginalia: Cursive styling (`Caveat`) for informal notes, arrows, and scan indicators.
  - Metadata & System Indicators: Monospace styling (`JetBrains Mono`) for contact chips, coordinate labels, and code specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model mimics an organized physical workspace. While the global container fits standard content widths, interior items support subtle rotational angles, sticker overlaps, and tactile pin points.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** Multi-column grid system (12 columns on desktop > 1024px, 6 columns on tablet, 1–2 columns on mobile) overlaid against an integrated mat texture (48px primary grid lines with 12px secondary subdivisions).
- **Z-Index Layering:** Content cards simulate physical layers: wooden desk backing (base) → blue cutting mat (canvas) → notepad paper sheets → clips/pins/stickers (top).
- **Staggered Angles:** Cards feature slight alternating rotations (`-3deg` to `+3deg`) with vertical offsets on large viewports, leveling to `0deg` on mobile viewports for compact horizontal alignment.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Floating stationery elements (rulers, pencils, clips) gracefully scale down or tuck beneath viewport boundaries under 768px to ensure unencumbered reading.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical sheet elevations combined with hard-edged stationery drop shadows instead of standard diffuse blurs.

### Elevation Levels & Depth Tokens
- **Mat Surface (Level 0):** Flat cutting mat base textured with crisp grid subdivisions and subtle noise grain.
- **Taped & Flat Sheets (Level 1):** Subtle physical drop `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)` for memo slips and stickers adhering directly to the mat surface.
- **Elevated Notepads & Cards (Level 2):** Elevated paper components utilize `0 14px 28px rgba(0,0,0,0.22)` combined with directional physical offsets.
- **Clipboard Heavy Platters (Level 3):** Wooden backboards and master containers carry deep dimensional depth `0 24px 48px rgba(0,0,0,0.32)`.
- **Hard Stationery Shadows:** Buttons, tags, software tiles, and bounding frames feature crisp offsets: `3px 3px 0px #18181B` or `4px 4px 0px #18181B`. Hover interactions compress this displacement to `1px 1px 0px #18181B`, simulating tactile physical buttons.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes rounded stationery forms contrasted with geometric paper craft cuts.

### Shape Characteristics
- **Standard Radius:** Base token `roundedness: 2` (8px standard corner, 16px for `rounded-lg`, 24px for `rounded-xl`). Used for ID badge cards, tool chips, and content frames.
- **Pill Geometry:** Complete radial capsule curves (`border-radius: 9999px`) applied to navigation bars, status strips, and metadata tag chips.
- **Craft Paper Treatments:**
  - Spiral Edges: Repetitive circular punching holes aligned along top borders.
  - Jagged / Torn Cuts: Angular polygonal clip-paths along bottom margins simulating hand-torn notepad leaves.
  - Sticky Note Corners: Asymmetric corner radii (`6px 6px 24px 6px`) to mimic lifted sheet corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Push Button:** Solid white or accent-yellow surface, 2px solid `#18181B` outline, hard offset shadow `4px 4px 0 #18181B`. Font: `Plus Jakarta Sans` 700. On hover: shifts down and right `2px`, shadow reduces to `2px 2px 0 #18181B`.
- **Status / Pill Action Button:** Dark translucent capsule (`rgba(15, 35, 65, 0.72)` or `#18181B`), white text, crisp borders, subtle hover glow.

### Interactive Cards & Sheets
- **Spiral Notepad Card:** White paper background (`#F8F7F3`), light blue graph lines, perforated ring punches at the top edge. Pinned with a simulated `#FF5CA8` binder clip.
- **Wooden Clipboard Card:** `#B88655` exterior with `#7C532E` thick border, 28px rounded corners, framing inner white sheets.
- **ID Lanyard Badge:** Yellow badge casing (`#FFD23F`) with top hanging strap, photo cutout, tilted 2–3 degrees with hard drop shadows.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pixel Badges:** Pixel font, solid black or bright pink fill with white text, 2px stroke, tight padding (`4px 8px`).
- **Soft Skill Tags:** Pill-shaped capsules (`roundedness: 3`), 1.5px border, subtle pastel backgrounds with ink-black typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Notepad Input Fields:** Ruled notebook baseline inputs with dashed lower borders (`2px dashed #1D63B8`) or clean pill inputs framed in 2px black outlines.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-drawn geometric square or circle containers with thick ink outlines; checked states display a solid interior fill or check doodle.

### App Tiles & Tool Icons
- Uniform `56px × 56px` square tiles, 14px corner radius, bold brand colors, and tactile `3px 3px 0 #18181B` shadows. Hover triggers an upward spring lift with tooltip displays.
